How it works

The deposit, explained

Your first bottle carries a ₹160 refundable security deposit — think of it as the bottle's value, held safe until you're done with it. From then on, every refill is a flat ₹160. Whenever you're ready to stop — moving out, switching things up, whatever the reason — just hand the empty bottle back and we return your full ₹160 deposit. No hidden charges, no questions asked.
